Two choices before the build

The pixel office in the Cozy style is the base. What is still open: who lives in it, and whether the project gets a second way of being seen.

1 · THE PEOPLE

Same six people in three designs. Each strip shows them standing, walking towards you, walking sideways and walking away.

2 · A SECOND WAY TO SEE IT

Not another coat of paint: four different ideas of what Agent Office is. Each one shows the same thing, four agents working and three resting.

The office cut open from the side, one room per floor, a lift between them. You scroll it like a feed.
