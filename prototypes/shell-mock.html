PUT YOUR OWN AGENTS IN THE OFFICE

Claude Code — one command, runs on your machine, sends nothing out: 
npx agent-office
Any other bot or script — tell the office when it works and when it stops: 
curl -X POST http://localhost:3737/api/event \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $OFFICE_TOKEN" \
  -d '{"agent":"my-bot","state":"working","note":"crunching numbers"}'
States: working · thinking · idle · done · error. The office shows a name, a mood and what the agent is up to. No file names, no project names, no prompts.

Drag to move, scroll to travel through the office, Ctrl + scroll or the buttons to zoom. Click an agent or the cat. The lighting, glow, camera and minimap run live on the frame; walking, Sims mode and games stay as they are in the real app.
