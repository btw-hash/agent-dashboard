WHY NOT ANOTHER PIXEL OFFICE

  • Pixel-art agent offices are already a crowded genre: Pixel Agents, Pixel Agent Desk, Claude Office Visualizer, pixtuoid, Clawffice. A sixth one disappears in that row.
  • Nothing here is a downloaded asset. Furniture and people are assembled from simple shapes in code, so the repo stays free to share and anyone can run it.
  • Real light does the heavy lifting: sun through the windows, long shadows at dusk, screens and neon at night.

WHAT CARRIES OVER

  • Agents sit down and type when they work, wander off when they rest.
  • All 18 games: click the arcade cabinet, the poker table, the dartboard and the game opens as before.
  • The cat, the goose, pizza at noon, cleaners at 23:00, party mode, Sims mode.
  • Four fun rooms beside the main hall, so the whole building fits one screen.

LITTLE THINGS ALREADY IN THIS SLICE

  • Mugs pile up next to whoever has worked longest; sticky notes collect on monitors.
  • Each desk has its own buddy: a rubber duck, a cactus, a trophy.
  • The wall clock shows your real time. Fish swim in the tank.
  • Code scrolls on every screen; the thinker stares at his.
  • Someone always falls asleep on the couch.

This slice is one room with seven people. The full build is the whole floor: main hall, kitchen, arcade, gym, casino and café, with up to twenty desks.
