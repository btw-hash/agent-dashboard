HOW IT BEHAVES ON A PHONE

  • One finger turns the room, two fingers zoom. The page still scrolls outside the scene.
  • Phone quality: smaller shadow map, no edge blur, capped resolution. Tap the button to preview it on a desktop.
  • On a tall screen the camera pulls back so the whole room fits the width.
  • In the full app a phone opens on the busiest desks and follows the action; the list of people becomes a bottom sheet.

WHAT CARRIES OVER

  • Agents sit down and type when they work, wander off when they rest.
  • All 18 games open from their objects, exactly as now.
  • The cat, the goose, pizza at noon, cleaners at 23:00, party mode, Sims mode.
  • Four fun rooms beside the main hall, so the building fits one screen.

LITTLE THINGS ALREADY HERE

  • Mugs pile up beside whoever has worked longest; sticky notes collect on monitors.
  • Each desk has its own buddy: a rubber duck, a cactus, a trophy.
  • The wall clock shows your real time. Fish swim in the tank.
  • Code scrolls on every screen. Someone always falls asleep on the couch.

This slice is one room with seven people. The full build is the whole floor: main hall, kitchen, arcade, gym, casino and café, with up to twenty desks.
