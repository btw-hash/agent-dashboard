THE WALK, FRAME BY FRAME

Six frames per direction, and the cycle advances with distance covered, so feet never slide.

WHAT CHANGED AGAINST TODAY

  • On a phone the office opens zoomed in on the desks and you drag to look around.
  • One pixel grid, whole-number zoom only. Today sprites are stretched by 1.25, which is where the blur comes from.
  • Every sprite gets a dark outline and a light and a shadow side.
  • Furniture has a front face and sits on the floor with a shadow instead of lying flat.
  • Heads are drawn on the grid, with real hair shapes, glasses, caps and headphones.
  • Wood floor, panelled wall and windows replace the grey noise.

STILL OURS

  • Same camera, same idea: desks, lounge, kitchen, arcade, the cat and the goose.
  • Same colours: navy walls, purple rug, the pink neon.
  • All current poses can be redrawn in this style, because people are still assembled from parts in code.
